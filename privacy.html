Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026
Zero Cloud Upload Architecture: All file conversions, video frame extractions, PDF parsings, and image zipping occur entirely inside your web browser’s sandboxed memory. Your files are never transmitted to, processed by, or stored on any remote servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because ZippaGif operates 100% on the client side:

  • We do not collect, view, or retain any videos, images, or documents you select for conversion.
  • We do not maintain user file accounts, databases, or cloud storage buckets.

2. Information Stored Locally in Your Browser

ZippaGif utilizes client-side web technologies to provide an offline and persistent experience:

  • Local Storage (localStorage): If you purchase and activate ZippaGif Pro, your license key is stored locally in your browser to maintain your unlocked Pro status across sessions.
  • Service Workers & Browser Caching: Core application assets (scripts, styles, web fonts) are cached on your local device to enable offline execution. No personal user data is cached.

3. Payment Processing & Order Information

All commercial transactions and software licensing are processed securely by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy, LLC. When purchasing a Pro license, billing details (such as your name, billing address, email, and payment card data) are collected directly by Lemon Squeezy in accordance with their privacy policy. ZippaGif never has access to your payment card details.

4. Analytics and Third-Party CDNs

ZippaGif loads open-source client libraries from established, reputable Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) including cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. These providers may process standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP addresses and browser user-agent headers) solely for delivering static assets and mitigating security threats.

5. Contact Information

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us via email at support@zippagif.com.
